---

HOW TO USE THE SHOPPING CART SYSTEM

---

STARTING THE PROGRAM

1. Run the program.
2. The menu will appear showing all available products.

---

ADDING ITEMS TO CART

1. Type the Product ID or Product Name.
2. Enter the quantity you want.
3. Choose:
   * Y to Continue shopping
   * N to Proceed to checkout

---

VIEW YOUR CART - Press 1
* Shows all items you added

---

CLEAR CART - Press 3
* Removes all items from your cart

---

REMOVE ITEM FROM CART - Press 4

1. Enter Product Name or ID
2. Enter quantity to remove

---

SEARCH PRODUCT - Press 5

1. Type:
   * Product name (`soda`)
   * OR category (`food`)
2. Matching products will be displayed

---

CHECKOUT - Press 2
Steps:
1. Confirm checkout (**Y/N**)
2. View order summary
3. Enter payment amount
4. Confirm payment

---

LOW STOCK ALERT
After checkout:
Items with 5 or less stock will be shown

---

SHOP AGAIN
* After checkout:
  * Y → Go back to menu
  * N → Exit program

---

EXIT PROGRAM - Press 6

---

INVALID INPUTS AND STOCK QUANTITIES

* Invalid inputs will show "INVALID INPUT
* You cannot buy more than available stock
* Cart resets after checkout
